# AI Effect Studio

AI Effect Studio is a browser-based photo-to-video tool for making short AI dance, meme, car, sports, and fashion clips. Choose a preset, preview the scene, and turn one to three photos into a downloadable MP4. You don't need to write a prompt or edit a timeline.

[Browse AI video effects on AI Effect Studio](https://aieffectstudio.com/)

## See what you can make

| Effect | Photos | Example result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Tiny Desk AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/tiny-desk-ai) | 1 or 3 | Put yourself in the lead singer's seat, or add two friends as the keyboard and sax players. |
| [Finger Swipe Reveal AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/finger-swipe-reveal-ai) | 2 | Make a two-person reveal where fingers slide away from the camera and both faces come into view. |
| [Glow Trio AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/glow-trio-ai) | 3 | Put three friends into a synchronized glowstick dance. |

Each example page shows a sample result and explains which photos work best.

## How it works

1. Preview an effect and check how many photos it needs.
2. Upload photos you have permission to use. For group effects, add one person's photo to each role.
3. Check the generation price, then generate and download your MP4.

**Can I preview an effect before paying?** Yes. You can watch the preset preview before uploading. Generating your own video is paid, and each effect page shows the cost before you generate.
