# AI Effect Studio

AI Effect Studio turns your photos into short videos you can share. Pick a scene you’d like to be in, from a desk concert to a dance with friends, and see a sample before you upload anything. The effect already has the setting, camera moves, and timing. You choose who appears in it, and AI Effect Studio makes a new video from your photos.

You can use AI Effect Studio right from your browser. Start with a photo you already have, without filming a clip, writing a prompt, or learning to edit video.

[Browse the effects and watch a preview](https://aieffectstudio.com/)

## What can you make with the photos you have?

**One portrait.** In [Tiny Desk AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/tiny-desk-ai), you’re the singer at the desk, wearing headphones and cracking up at the mic. A clear photo where you’re facing the camera works well for that close-up. Choose Trio if you want two friends in the band as the keyboard and sax players.

**Two separate photos.** Try [Finger Swipe Reveal AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/finger-swipe-reveal-ai) with a friend or partner. The video opens with two fingers hiding your eyes, then they swipe away and both of you burst out laughing. The reveal is a close-up, so sharp photos from the shoulders up work better than distant full-body shots.

**Three photos for a group dance.** In [Glow Trio AI](https://aieffectstudio.com/glow-trio-ai), you and two friends swing glowsticks through a dance routine in a dim studio. Pick who goes in the middle, on the left, and on the right. For this one, photos that show each person from the knees up or farther back give the effect more to work with.

## How to make your first video

1. **Watch the preview.** Open the effect page to see how the scene plays out. Check the number of photos, video length, and format so you know what you’ll get.
2. **Add your photos.** Start with a sharp, evenly lit photo where the face is easy to see. Skip sunglasses and heavy filters if you have another shot handy. For a duet or group effect, use a separate photo for each role and make sure everyone is happy to be included.
3. **Review the options and generate.** Check the price shown on the effect page. If there’s a choice between 480p and 720p, 480p can be enough for a quick share in a group chat. Pick 720p if you want finer detail for a public post.
4. **Come back for the MP4.** Most videos are ready in a few minutes. You can close the page while it’s being made and find the result under **My videos** when you come back.

## Before you make a video

**Can I try it for free?** You can watch the sample videos for free. Generating a video with your own photos is paid. The effect page shows the cost for your selection before you generate. If a generation is confirmed to have failed, any credits used for it are returned automatically. [See pricing options](https://aieffectstudio.com/pricing)

**Will the result look exactly like the preview?** The preview shows the scene and timing. Your photos determine who appears in the new video, and details such as expressions can vary. The sample results on each effect page let you compare a photo with what the effect made from it.

**Who can see my photos and videos?** Uploads are stored privately and deleted after 7 days. Finished videos are visible in your account for up to 30 days, so download anything you want to keep. [Read the privacy policy](https://aieffectstudio.com/privacy)
